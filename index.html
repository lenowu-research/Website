Undergraduate research experience

How my undergraduate work evolved and led to tendon health monitoring.

My undergraduate path moved from additive manufacturing and hardware testing into acoustics, experimental validation, and finally a biomechanics-focused capstone centered on wearable ultrasound and injury-risk monitoring.

Oct 2022 - Dec 2023

Purdue Additive and Intelligent Manufacturing Lab

Foundation

Built my early research foundation through additive manufacturing, optical characterization, CAD modeling, and publication work on 3D printed diffraction gratings.

Advisor: Professor Huachao Mao and Dr. Yujie Shan

Publication: Hua, J., Shan, Y., Wu, S., and Mao, H. (2023). Three-Dimensional Printed Diffraction Gratings Drop Coated by Different Resins and Their Mechanism. Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering, 146(1), 011001.

Jun 2023 - Aug 2023

Aptiv Shanghai Test Center

Industry

Strengthened my engineering practice through modal analysis, durability testing, materials evaluation, and simulation for power distribution box design.

May 2024 - Aug 2025

Purdue Ray W. Herrick Labs

Acoustics

Expanded into acoustic modeling and experimental validation using COMSOL, MATLAB, prototype fabrication, and water-tank testing for underwater noise mitigation.

Advisor: Dr. Junfei Li

Publication: Zhang, Y., Wu, S. (Co-First Author), Li, R., Oh, H., Liu, Y., and Li, J. (2026). Foldable acoustic metamaterial for underwater broadband low-frequency noise mitigation. Physical Review Applied, 25(2), 024029.

Aug 2025 - Dec 2025

Tendon Health Monitoring System

Biomechanics

My most important undergraduate project. I led development of an integrated ankle brace and ultrasound system for real-time monitoring of Achilles tendon fatigue and injury risk.

Wearable design, ultrasound sensing, MATLAB signal processing, GUI development, and team leadership.
